About Us

A family-friendly fast food and local Pakistani restaurant in the heart of Karachi.

Authentic Taste, Modern Experience

Bilal Restaurant is a family-friendly fast food and local Pakistani restaurant located in the heart of Karachi. We take pride in serving fresh, hygienic, and delicious dishes that remind you of home.

Our chefs are experts in traditional recipes — whether it's the rich, creamy Chicken Karahi, the perfectly spiced Biryani, or our crispy fast food items.

Visit us for an unforgettable dining experience or call us for delivery. We are committed to quality and customer satisfaction.
